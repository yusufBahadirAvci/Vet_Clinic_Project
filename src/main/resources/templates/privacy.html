Privacy notice

Information about the processing of personal data under the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Data controller

VetClinic, Example Street 1, Istanbul (info@vetclinic.example).

Personal data we process

  • Identity and contact data: full name, e-mail address and/or phone number.
  • Pet data linked to you: your pets' details and vaccination records.
  • Adoption applications: the details and message you submit with an application.
  • Login data: one-time login codes (stored only as a hash) and their usage times.

Why we process it

  • To provide veterinary services and keep your pets' vaccination cards.
  • To send you one-time login codes and vaccination reminders.
  • To evaluate adoption applications and to register the new owner when an adoption is approved.

Legal basis and consent

We process your data with your explicit consent, which we ask for when you are registered at the clinic or when you submit an adoption application, and where it is necessary to perform our services.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your data and share it with no third party other than the e-mail and SMS services needed to deliver our messages. We keep your data for as long as you are a client of the clinic or as required by law.

Your rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you may ask whether your data is processed, request information about it, ask for corrections or deletion, and withdraw your consent. To use these rights, contact us at the e-mail address above.
